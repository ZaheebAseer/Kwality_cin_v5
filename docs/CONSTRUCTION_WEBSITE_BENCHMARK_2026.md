# Construction Website Benchmark — 2026

## Scope
This review compares the current Kwality Interiors landing experience against representative current websites from major construction/infrastructure firms and established AEC digital patterns. It is not an exhaustive survey of every construction website worldwide.

## Patterns observed

### 1. Project proof is central
Bechtel, Turner, DPR, Skanska, Laing O'Rourke and Balfour Beatty all make project portfolios a major part of the customer-facing experience. Their project systems commonly organize work by market/sector, location, expertise or scope. Kwality should therefore make real project/work photography the strongest evidence module once supplied.

### 2. Capabilities are organized around customer problems
Leading firms do not rely on a single giant list of services. They frame capabilities through sectors, project types, expertise and delivery methods. Kwality should keep its verified services, but present them as visual chapters rather than a dashboard-like grid.

### 3. Trust comes from evidence and delivery language
Large contractors pair portfolio proof with people, safety, quality, innovation, delivery approach and contact paths. Kwality should use an evidence-led trust stack: credentials, verified client relationships, a simple typical engagement flow, and real work imagery when available.

### 4. Contact is intentionally easy
Top contractor sites make it easy to contact a regional or project team. A single-page site for Kwality should keep the conversion path shorter: phone, WhatsApp, email and one lightweight enquiry form.

### 5. Technology is subordinate to the story
Leading construction sites increasingly use interactive/3D/digital techniques, but the core experience remains project-, market- and customer-led. Kwality's 3D viewer should stay as a supporting structural visualization, not become the website itself.

### 6. Remove corporate features that do not fit V1
Careers, investor relations, newsrooms, long-form insight libraries, large office maps and multi-country directories are useful for global enterprises but do not belong on this two-stage commercial landing page.

## V5 decisions

### Keep
- one-page commercial narrative
- construction-first hero
- industrial shed visual chapter
- project/work proof
- Premier Energies experience
- credentials
- selective 3D structural viewer
- contact conversion
- strong mobile CTA
- semantic HTML and SEO foundations

### Add
- stronger project-proof emphasis
- typical engagement flow
- visual market/industry scenes
- document/evidence framing
- one-page enquiry form that can hand off to WhatsApp
- clearer visual hierarchy between construction, fabrication, finishing and architectural systems
- explicit distinction between representative imagery and real business evidence

### Reduce/remove
- dashboard-like service tabs as the primary service presentation
- repetitive card grids
- unnecessary numeric/statistical claims
- unsupported technical claims such as exact material grades, performance ratings or project counts
- generic corporate content that does not help a customer decide to enquire

## Reference sites reviewed
- Bechtel — projects, markets, regions, services, safety, quality and sustainability.
- Turner Construction — project portfolio organized by location, scope and industry.
- DPR Construction — construction purpose, markets, expertise, projects and contact pathways.
- Skanska — purpose, markets, world-class projects and global portfolio browsing.
- Laing O'Rourke — projects/sectors plus delivery model, health and safety, modern methods and specialist businesses.
- Balfour Beatty — expertise, sectors, project portfolio and safety/innovation.
- Mortenson — project-led proof, market positioning and contact/career pathways.
