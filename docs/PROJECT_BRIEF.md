## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**Kwality Interiors** is an established Indian construction and industrial services firm based in Rajendra Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana. Founded in 2022, the company specializes in civil construction, structural fabrication, industrial shed erection, protective and specialized coatings, precision glass works, uPVC systems, and aluminium architectural structures.

The mission of this project is to build an **exceptional, production-grade commercial website** that positions Kwality Interiors as an elite, dependable, and technically capable contractor for industrial facilities, solar manufacturers, infrastructure developers, and corporate enterprises across Telangana and broader industrial hubs in India.

### Core Philosophy: The Hybrid Commercial Web Experience
This is **NOT** a 3D tech demo or a full-canvas WebGL toy. It is an authoritative commercial website designed to generate real business inquiries, validate high-value contracts, and build unflinching trust with enterprise clients (such as Premier Energies and SNR Electricals). 

It strategically blends:
1. **Semantic, high-performance HTML & CSS** for lightning-fast delivery and technical SEO.
2. **Dominant cinematic photography & video** for industrial authority and visual immersion.
3. **Subtle motion & GSAP scroll choreography** to depict scale and structural flow.
4. **Selective, isolated 3D viewports** only where spatial manipulation and structural engineering details add tangible comprehension value.

### V1 Route Scope: Strictly One Single Landing Page (`/`)
- Public Routes: Strictly `/` (No separate `/contact` route; contact lives at `#contact`).
- Information Architecture: 13 sequential visual chapters taking visitors from the cinematic hero through the 4 service divisions, verified client track record, and down to the final RFQ conversion station.
